Featured Section

A network for collective climate action

CLAN is building a space where young people can explore, co-create and actively engage in climate action.

Our Story: The Climate Leaders Action Network (CLAN) is a flexible and responsive platform where young people come together to explore, connect, co-create, strengthen, and actively engage in climate action.

Initially formed by individuals who completed the Climate Leadership Program (CLP), CLAN is now active across four states with a growing network of over 100 members. The network is expanding its membership to welcome a wider circle of young people committed to advancing community-centred climate solutions.

Our Vision

A just, resilient and regenerative future where young people are empowered to shape climate action, strengthen communities, and create a more sustainable world together.

Our Mission

To connect, equip and support young people to understand climate challenges, collaborate with communities and diverse stakeholders, share knowledge, and take meaningful collective action for a just and climate-resilient future.

Pillars of Action

What We're Working On

CLAN's activities take the form of campaigns, local initiatives, and project collaborations shaped by members' interests and community needs.

Engagements with Local Self-Governments

Promoting youth participation in local climate governance, and creating space for young people to engage directly with the institutions that shape climate decisions.

Engagements with Children

Building climate awareness and resilience among younger generations, through hands-on work with schools and the communities around them.

Climate and Communications

Enhancing storytelling, public outreach, media engagement, and capacity building among CLAN members to amplify youth voices.

Open Data and Knowledge Sharing

Creating accessible, data-driven resources so climate action across the network — and far beyond it — can be more informed and effective.
